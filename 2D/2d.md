of the Laplace-Beltrami operator on a human face

## 🌿 The Mathematics Written in Reality

HST reads a language that nature has always spoken.

The Laplace-Beltrami operator describes how heat flows across any surface.
Its eigenfunctions — the harmonic notes — reveal the intrinsic structure
of that surface, independent of how it is embedded, measured, or observed.

I discovered that this structure is present not only in 3D meshes,
but in photographs of living organic matter.

A human face captured in a photograph produces the same clean, smooth
harmonic gradient as a FAUST 3D scan. The eigenfunction reads the skin —
the continuous, organic, differentiable geometry of a living surface —
and finds the same axis, the same fingerprint, the same note.

**A box does not have this property.** A chair does not. A monitor does not.
These are human constructs — their geometry is discontinuous, their
edges are sharp, their surfaces are flat. The Laplacian finds nothing to read.

But a face, a body, a tree, a wave — these are *solutions*.
Solutions to the equations that govern physical reality.
And HST recognizes them.

> *"The harmonic note belongs to every shape found in nature.  
> It does not belong to things we invented."*

This is the mathematics that describes this reality

This is not a limitation of HST. It is a precise statement about the
